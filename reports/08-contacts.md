## Decision-makers and program owners

**Department of War**
- **Brent G. Ingraham**, Assistant Secretary of the Army (ALT) — Army acquisition executive over the JIATF-401 IDIQs and drones.mil.
- **Dan Driscoll**, Secretary of the Army — sets the cost-curve narrative; engage only through program offices.
- **Lt. Gen. Francisco Lozano**, PAE Fires and RCCTO director (nominated) — owns C-UAS programs of record (Coyote/LIDS, E-HEL, IFPC-HPM). Contracting runs through Army Contracting Command–Redstone (W31P4Q).
- **BG Guy Yelverton**, Capability Portfolio Executive, Defensive Fires — Low-Cost Interceptor program.
- **Col. Jason Idleman**, Chief, Multi-Domain Operations Division, JIATF-401; **Michelle Self**, Deputy, Rapid Acquisition Division, JIATF-401.
- **Gen. Gregory Guillot**, Commander NORTHCOM/NORAD — homeland C-UAS synchronizer; owns the fly-away kit.
- **BG Curtis King**, CG 10th AAMDC — Project Flytrap host in Europe with the 52nd ADA Brigade.
- **Owen West**, Director DIU; **David Payne**, acting director of DIU's Autonomous Warfare portfolio (which includes counter-UAS). DIU is the designated front door for commercial unmanned and autonomous companies ([June 2026](https://www.diu.mil/latest/defense-innovation-unit-announces-new-director-designation-as-defense-field)).
- **DRPM-UxS** — new single integrator for unmanned and counter-unmanned systems, reporting to Deputy Secretary Steve Feinberg. Director not yet named.
- **Marine Corps PM Ground Based Air Defense** (PEO Land Systems) — MADIS/L-MADIS; Col. Andrew Konicki was PM as of 2024 (verify).
- **ACC Point Defense Battle Lab**, 319th Reconnaissance Wing, Grand Forks AFB — domestic base C-sUAS evaluations; asked for drone-on-drone interceptors in July 2026.

**Homeland**
- **Markwayne Mullin**, Secretary of Homeland Security (Kristi Noem held the post in January 2026).
- **Pedro Allende**, Under Secretary for S&T — runs the $1.5B C-UAS IDIQ.
- **Justin Napolitano**, DHS Chief Security Officer — department-wide C-UAS requirements.
- **FEMA Grant Programs Directorate** — C-UAS Grant Program; FEMA Grants News 800-368-6498. Buyers are the State Administrative Agencies (for example Cal OES: `C-UASGrantProgram@caloes.ca.gov`).
- **FBI National Counter-UAS Training Center**, Huntsville — certifies every state and local mitigation operator.
- **Kash Patel**, FBI Director.

**Congress**

- **Rep. Rick Larsen**, House T&I Ranking Member — the aviation-safety brake on domestic mitigation.
- House and Senate Appropriations defense subcommittees — the JIATF-401 line ($1.4B House mark).

**Allies**
