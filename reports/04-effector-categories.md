# Report 4 — Contracts by effector: lasers, microwave, missiles, guns, RF and sensors

*Categories outside UM's own lane, summarized for context: where the money goes, what each category costs, and where it can legally be used. Interceptor drones and capture are covered in Report 5.*

## Missiles and rockets — the biggest single programs

- **Raytheon Coyote / LIDS:** $5.04B (Sep 2025, through 2033) plus earlier awards ($237M in 2023; $75M for 600 Block 2C in Jan 2024; $197M in Sep 2024). The Army sought FY27 money for a [multi-year deal](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-13/us-looks-to-ensure-long-term-supply-of-rtx-drone-interceptors). Raytheon is #1 in the federal ledger ($2.18B obligated).
- **Next Generation C-sUAS Missile (NGCM):** RFI Aug 2026; under $150K each, 5,000+, Coyote-launcher compatible ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/08/army-launches-search-for-counter-drone-missile-priced-under-150k/)).
- **Low-Cost Interceptor:** under $250K (flexible), live fire at White Sands in fall 2026; xTech|Apex Intercept $8M prize pool.
- **APKWS:** BAE dual-mode air-to-air, $145M sole source (Feb 2026); L3Harris VAMPIRE, Army up to $106M (Jun 2026) and a Navy selection (Sep 2026).
- **AV Freedom Eagle FE-1:** $95.9M LRKI (Oct 2025). **Lockheed Grizzly:** JIATF-401 pick (Sep 2026).
- **Navy NAIP:** containerized launcher of 60+ interceptors, capability within 12 months of award (white papers Sep 2026).

*Homeland use:* generally excluded. NORTHCOM named Coyote as [not appropriate for the homeland](https://www.twz.com/air/lasers-microwaves-missiles-guns-not-on-the-table-for-domestic-drone-defense) in 2024.

## High-energy lasers — production has started

- **US:** AV LOCUST E-HEL, $464.8M OTA (Sep 2026), called the first US HEL production contract; earlier AMP-HEL/P-HEL (BlueHalo), DE M-SHORAD (Kord/Raytheon, $203M OTA, 2019), HELIOS (Lockheed, $150M, 2018), HELWS (Raytheon, 2019).
- **UK:** DragonFire £316M (Nov 2025) at about £10 a shot. **Israel:** Iron Beam (NIS 2B; first unit Dec 2025, not fully operational by Aug 2026). **South Korea:** Block-I (~$72.5M, 2024) at about $1.45 a shot. **Japan:** KHI and MHI vehicle lasers. **Australia:** AIM Fractl.

*Homeland use:* excluded (hazard to aircraft and people). *Cost per shot:* the lowest of any category, but systems cost millions and are limited by weather and line of sight.

## High-power microwave

- **Epirus Leonidas:** $66.1M (Jan 2023), Gen II $43.6M (Jul 2025), USMC ExDECS (2025) and HAVOC $11M (Aug 2026). **Leidos Mjölnir** $26.9M (2022). Epirus is the clearest SBIR-to-program story in the ledger.

*Homeland use:* excluded (emitting into populated areas).

## Guns and smart fire control

- **Allen Control Systems Bullfrog:** $500M JIATF-401 IDIQ (Sep 2026), Army SBIR-derived contract (Jan 2026), South Korea and UAE; $2.2B valuation.
- **SmartShooter SMASH:** $150M JIATF-401 IDIQ; DLA catalogue buys of SMASH 2000L.
- **Rheinmetall Skyranger / Skynex:** Germany €595M; Netherlands; Belgium (20 within the €3.1B package); Ukraine.
- **Kongsberg:** MADIS RS6 ($94M ceiling, 2021); Poland SAN (35/30/12.7 mm guns).

*Homeland use:* excluded (where the rounds land).

## RF / EW defeat and cyber takeover — the homeland default

- **DroneShield:** $500M JIATF-401 IDIQ; JIATF-401 order (A$24.9M); European orders of US$61.6M and A$49.6M; DLA DroneSentry-X.
- **CACI SkyValor:** $500M JIATF-401 (Jul 2026).
- **AV Titan:** $500M JIATF-401 (Jul 2026), first order $80.6M.
- **D-Fend EnforceAir2:** DLA kits ($21–24M orders 2025–26), Secret Service, DEA; Motorola buying D-Fend for $1.5B.
- **DroneBuster:** hundreds of DLA, Army and Air Force buys since 2017.
- **Taiwan:** 635 portable sets (~$312M).

*Homeland use:* allowed (jamming needs advance approval; cyber takeover is approved). It is also the only mitigation expected on the first SLTT technology list. *Weakness:* useless against drones that don't emit or receive RF, which is UM's core claim.

## Sensing and detection

- **JIATF-401 IDIQ:** Echodyne ($250M), RADA ($500M), L3Harris WESCAM ($500M), PVP Advanced EO ($250M).
- **State Department:** Echodyne ($35.3M) and RADA ($9M) radars, 2025.
- **Hidden Level:** airspace monitoring subscription. **Denmark:** Weibel radars. **HENSOLDT:** ESSI.
- **DIU:** Counter-UAS sensing CSO for homeland and mobile defense (Feb 2026).

## C2 and integration

- **Anduril Lattice** became the C-UAS C2: $20B enterprise vehicle, $87M first task order. FAAD-C2 (Northrop) remains in the Army air-defense stack. The **US-UK C-UAS data standard** (Mar 2026) is becoming a marketplace requirement.
