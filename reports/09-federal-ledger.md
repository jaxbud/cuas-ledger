# Report 9 — The federal ledger: 921 US government C-UAS awards since 2007

*Source: USAspending.gov public API, harvested 3 October 2026 using 70+ counter-drone search terms across contracts, IDVs, grants and other awards, then filtered to awards whose description or recipient clearly concerns counter-UAS. Each row links to its USAspending record.*

## What this ledger is, and what it isn't

- **It is** the most complete public record of *money actually obligated* by US federal agencies on counter-drone work: 921 awards totalling about **$5.45B obligated** from FY2011 to FY2026.
- **It isn't** a list of contract ceilings. A $500M IDIQ appears here only as the orders placed on it. The $7B JIATF-401 program, the $20B Anduril vehicle and the $1.5B DHS IDIQ show up as small obligations or not yet at all.
- **It lags.** DoD contract data reaches USAspending on a delay of about 90 days, so FY2026 ($484.6M so far) is understated.
- **It misses some Other Transaction agreements** and classified work, and it only catches awards whose text uses a recognizable counter-drone term. Treat the totals as a floor.

## Who gets paid

Raytheon ($2.18B) dwarfs everyone else through Coyote and KuRFS. Next: SRC ($485M), Northrop Grumman ($238M), Leonardo DRS ($205M), Black River Systems ($187M), Atlantic Diving Supply ($155M, a DLA distributor), Leidos ($139M), SAIC ($137M), Anduril ($131M), Booz Allen ($129M), CACI ($94M) and AeroVironment ($81M).

## What a startup can learn from the ledger

- **Distributors are a real channel.** DLA buys DroneBuster, EnforceAir2, SMASH and Dedrone through Atlantic Diving Supply, W.S. Darley and Noble. Once a product has a NSN or catalogue listing, units can buy it with little ceremony.
- **SBIR is a small door that opens wide.** The ledger holds 24 SBIR/STTR C-UAS awards (about $12.4M). Epirus took STTR and SBIR Phase I awards from 2019 onward, then won the [$66.1M IFPC-HPM award](https://www.epirusinc.com/news-item/us-army-awards-epirus-661m-contract-for-leonidas-directed-energy-system) in 2023. Allen Control Systems' 2026 Army contract came off SBIR topic A254-P030.
- **Civilian agencies buy small and often.** DEA, US Marshals, Secret Service, BOP and USDA each placed orders for Dedrone, D-Fend and DroneShield kits, usually under $1M.
- **Construction and installation follow sensors.** Several awards are for towers, airfield C-sUAS facilities and range modifications. Installation work is a predictable follow-on wherever C-UAS gets fielded.
