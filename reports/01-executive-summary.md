# Report 1 — Executive summary

*CUAS Ledger, 3 October 2026. Ten reports, 122 curated contracts and programmes, 921 federal awards from USAspending, 37 government statements, 30 contacts, 15 opportunities and 32 milestones. Every figure links to a source.*

## The market in one paragraph

Counter-drone contracting has gone from emergency buys (2016–2019) to consolidated programs (2019–2022) to mass procurement (2022–2025), and in the past year to enterprise-scale vehicles and homeland defense. US federal agencies have obligated at least **$5.45B** on counter-UAS since FY2011. Obligations roughly doubled from FY2021 to FY2025. Announced contract ceilings in calendar 2026 alone exceed **$53B worldwide** ([Unmanned Airspace](https://unmannedairspace.info/counter-uas-systems-and-policies/global-spending-on-c-uas-systems-reaches-more-than-usd53-billion-in-the-first-eight-months-of-2026)). The Pentagon's FY2027 request is **$20.6B for counter-unmanned systems**, 424% above FY2026 ([DoW Comptroller](https://comptroller.war.gov/Portals/45/Documents/defbudget/FY2027/FY2027_Budget_Request_Overview_Book.pdf)).

## Ten things to know

1. **JIATF-401 is the buyer.** It replaced the JCO in August 2025 and now runs a $7B multi-vendor IDIQ (12 holders), the cuas.mil marketplace, Domestic Shield and Replicator 2. The House FY27 bill gives it $1.4B, including a new $580M prototyping line.
2. **The biggest single vehicle is Anduril's $20B,** a 10-year Army agreement that makes Lattice the C-UAS command-and-control standard.
3. **Interceptor drones are now bought in bulk:** 13,000 Merops in 8 days; a $500M Perennial IDIQ; 590,000 Ukrainian UAVs ordered in 2026 with interceptors the top category; German, Latvian, UK and Australian interceptor contracts.
4. **Capture has a seat at the table.** The first Replicator 2 buy was Fortem's net-capture DroneHunter, picked for the homeland. Fortem is also on the Army ($18M) and DHS ($1.5B) vehicles.
5. **Homeland defense is now its own budget line:** Domestic Shield, FEMA's $500M, DHS's $1.5B IDIQ and a new DHS PEO, NNSA site protection, and the World Cup (300+ drones taken down).
6. **Authority keeps widening, with gates.** Commanders can now act beyond the fence line (Dec 2025). State and local police got authority in SAFER SKIES (Dec 2025), but the first authorized technology list is expected to be RF-only.
7. **Directed energy has reached production:** E-HEL ($464.8M), DragonFire (£316M), Iron Beam.
8. **Europe is building a drone wall:** Poland $4.2B, Belgium/Netherlands €3.1B, EU initiative targeting end-2026.
9. **Price is the battlefield:** Merops $15K going to $3–5K; Ukrainian interceptors about $2,500; the Army's missile programs target under $150K–$250K.
10. **Near-term money is constrained.** The CR runs to 11 December 2026 and bars new production starts. SBIR is back (reauthorized April 2026 to 2031, with a new $30M Phase II).

## For Unified Mechanics (detail in Report 10)

- **Fit:** strong. JIATF-401 asked for non-explosive homeland interceptors, the Air Force battle lab asked for drone-on-drone interceptors, and CBO lists entanglement as a defeat method.
- **Readiness:** not yet. Buyers select on test data, C2 integration and marketplace vetting.
- **First dollars:** the JIATF-401 Commercial Solutions Opening (rolling through 2028), the USASOC open call (1 Dec 2026), SBIR, xTech, and test venues (Air Force Point Defense Battle Lab, Navy Thunderdome). Then marketplace listing and teaming with IDIQ holders.
- **Biggest risks:** Fortem's position in capture, Anduril's Anvil and Lattice, interceptor price compression, the RF-only SLTT list, and budget timing.

## Method and limits

- **Sources:** primary documents where available (Army.mil, DVIDS, FEMA, Federal Register, DSCA, USAspending, company filings), then established trade press (Breaking Defense, DefenseScoop, Defense News, The War Zone, Inside Unmanned Systems, Unmanned Airspace, Janes summaries). Vendor press releases are labelled VENDOR.
- **"Every contract ever" is not literally reachable.** Classified awards, most OT agreements, foreign contracts with no public notice, and small purchases outside USAspending's keyword reach are missing. The federal ledger is a floor. The curated list covers every significant publicly reported award found during this sweep.
- **Values mix ceilings, obligations and estimates.** Each is labelled, and they are never added together on this site except where a source did so.
