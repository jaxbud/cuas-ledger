# Report 6 — Deep dive: every major C-UAS contract, October 2025 to October 2026

*Window: 1 October 2025 (start of US FY2026) to 3 October 2026. 85 curated awards and programmes, plus the federal ledger. Values are what the source reported; ceilings, obligations and estimates are labelled and never added together as if they were the same thing.*

## The year in five points

1. **The US moved from pilots to enterprise vehicles.** In twelve months the Army and JIATF-401 created a [$20B Anduril C2 enterprise vehicle](https://defensescoop.com/2026/03/14/anduril-20-billion-dollar-army-contract/), a [$7B multi-vendor IDIQ](https://www.army.mil/article/295771/jiatf_401_advances_domestic_shield_with_counter_uas_contract_awards) with 12 holders so far, and a [government marketplace](https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/08/10/pentagon-launches-online-marketplace-for-counter-uas-technology/). DHS added a [$1.5B department-wide IDIQ](https://insideunmannedsystems.com/dhs-awards-1-5-billion-counter-uas-contract-to-12-companies/).
2. **Interceptor drones became a purchased category.** The Army bought [13,000 Merops in eight days](https://www.armytimes.com/unmanned/2026/04/20/us-army-turns-to-ukraine-tested-drones-to-counter-iranian-uav-threat/) when the Iran war began, then JIATF-401 gave Perennial Autonomy a [$500M IDIQ](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/05/20/us-counter-drone-perennial-autonomy/). Germany, the UK, Latvia, Australia and the Gulf states all bought interceptors.
3. **Homeland defense got its own money.** Domestic Shield, the [$250M FEMA World Cup grants](https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20251230/historic-announcement-fema-rapidly-awards-250-million-secure-us-skies-ahead), [DHS's $115M](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/dhs-drone-office-counter-uas-funding) and [JIATF-401's $100M for the World Cup](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2026/04/mil-260406-dodnews02.htm) all went to protecting US sites and events.
4. **Directed energy reached production.** AV's [E-HEL laser award ($464.8M)](https://www.avinc.com/2026/09/02/avs-locust-selected-for-nearly-500-million-army-counter-uas-contract-for-enduring-high-energy-laser-e-hel-program/) is described as the first US HEL production contract. The UK put [DragonFire into production (£316M)](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/boost-for-armed-forces-as-new-laser-weapon-takes-down-high-speed-drones) and Israel [delivered Iron Beam](https://www.mod.gov.il/en/press-releases/press-room/major-milestone-in-high-power-laser-intercept-system-development).
5. **Europe built a drone wall.** Poland signed the [$4.2B SAN programme](https://www.edrmagazine.eu/kongsberg-wins-nok-16-billion-contract-to-deliver-counter-uas-solutions-in-poland); Belgium and the Netherlands a [€3.1B layered package](https://militaeraktuell.at/en/belgium-is-investing-3-1-billion-in-air-defense/); the EU set a drone-defence initiative for [initial capability by end-2026](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/10/15/eu-plans-functional-drone-wall-against-russia-by-end-of-2027-leak-shows).

- **Layered systems (19):** the largest by value — national programmes, enterprise IDIQs, DHS, NNSA ([$140M to Anduril for four sites](https://www.exchangemonitor.com/nnsa-awards-anduril-140-million-contract-for-counter-drone-systems/)).
- **Interceptor drones (13):** Perennial ($500M + $5.2M), TYTAN (Germany), Quantum Systems (15,000 for Ukraine's National Guard), Origin BLAZE (Latvia, Estonia, Belgium), Cambridge Aerospace Skyhammer (UK), UK LCADE (£3.16M to three SMEs), SYPAQ Corvo Strike (Australia), Alpine Eagle (Germany plus three), Ukrainian interceptors for the Gulf.
- **Grants (12):** all twelve FEMA World Cup/NCR awards, from $34.6M (California) to $5.3M (Kansas).
- **RF / EW defeat (8):** DroneShield ($500M IDIQ plus European orders of US$61.6M and A$49.6M), CACI SkyValor, AV Titan, Taiwan's 635 portable sets.
- **Capture (5):** Fortem (Replicator 2, Army $18M, World Cup), OpenWorks SkyWall (Army), Airobotics Iron Drone Raider (European NATO customer). **This is UM's lane, and every capture award in the window went to a net system.**
- **Missiles & rockets (5):** BAE APKWS dual-mode ($145M), L3Harris VAMPIRE (Army up to $106M; Navy selection), AV Freedom Eagle FE-1 ($95.9M), Lockheed Grizzly.
- **Guns & smart fire control (6), sensing (6), C2 (4), lasers (3), HPM (1: Epirus HAVOC, $11M), R&D (2).**

## Patterns that matter for a new entrant

- **IDIQ ceilings are not revenue.** The 29 September awards carried about $50M of obligations against $4.15B of ceilings ([DefenseScoop](https://defensescoop.com/2026/09/29/pentagon-task-force-army-announce-billions-in-counter-drone-tech-awards/)). Being on the vehicle is what counts; orders follow later.
- **Incumbents and specialists share the vehicles.** The ten new JIATF-401 holders mix large incumbents (L3Harris, SRC, Leonardo DRS's RADA) with smaller specialists, including venture-backed Allen Control Systems, valued at [$2.2B after its May 2026 raise](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260526638233/en/Allen-Control-Systems-Raises-%24200-Million-Series-B-at-%242.2-Billion-Post-Money-Valuation-to-Scale-Manufacturing-and-Accelerate-Deployment-of-Bullfrog).
- **Combat proof beats specs.** Merops (Ukraine), VAMPIRE (Ukraine) and DroneShield (Ukraine and Europe) were bought because of field use.
- **Performance failures do get cancelled.** Taiwan moved to [cancel a $30.5M fixed-site contract](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/07/20/taiwan-drop-drone-deal/) after repeated test failures.
