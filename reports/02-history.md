# Report 2 — Notable history: how counter-drone contracting got here (2015 – September 2025)

*The older record, compressed to what still shapes buying today. A sourced timeline, with 34 milestones, is on the History page.*

## Phase 1 — Urgent fixes (2015–2019)

Counter-drone buying started as a battlefield emergency. In Mosul in 2016, ISIS flew $2,000 quadcopters with 40 mm grenades. SOCOM's commander called armed drones [the most daunting problem of the year](https://defensenews.com/digital-show-dailies/sofic/2017/05/16/socom-commander-armed-isis-drones-were-2016s-most-daunting-problem). The response was a series of joint urgent operational needs:

- **SRC LIDS ($65M, Feb 2017)**, the root of the Army's fixed and expeditionary LIDS family ([Military & Aerospace Electronics](https://www.militaryaerospace.com/uncrewed/article/16726389/army-chooses-src-to-design-and-build-counter-drone-systems-to-destroy-or-disable-enemy-uavs)), followed by SRC's $57.5M Air Force supplies action (2018) and a $108M Army award.
- **ELTA North America** letter contracts for Air Force C-UAS supplies ($35.8M, Nov 2017; USAspending).
- **Raytheon KuRFS radar and Coyote interceptor** engineering tasks through DMEA's ATSP4 contract from 2017 (USAspending).
- **Lockheed Martin** quick-reaction C-UAS mode for the AN/TPQ-53 radar ($23.4M, Nov 2016; USAspending).
- **Lasers start:** Navy HELIOS ($150M, Mar 2018) and AFRL's Raytheon HELWS dune-buggy laser (2019, three prototypes, deployed overseas in 2020).
- **Leonardo DRS M-LIDS** down-select (Mar 2019; up to $190M IDIQ, Jul 2020) and **Kord / Raytheon DE M-SHORAD** 50 kW Stryker lasers ($203M OTA, Jul 2019).

At home, the 2018 **Gatwick shutdown** (about 1,000 flights) made airport drone defense a global issue. Congress gave DHS and DOJ counter-drone authority in the **Preventing Emerging Threats Act of 2018** (6 U.S.C. 124n); DoD already had 10 U.S.C. 130i (FY2017 NDAA).

## Phase 2 — Consolidation (2019–2022)

With about 40 overlapping systems in the field, DoD made the Army its executive agent (Nov 2019). The **Joint Counter-small UAS Office** picked an [interim set in June 2020](https://www.army.mil/article/236713/army_announces_selection_of_interim_c_suas_systems): FS-LIDS, NINJA and CORIAN (fixed); L-MADIS (mobile); Bal Chatri, Drone Buster and Smart Shooter (dismounted); FAAD-C2 with ADSI and MEDUSA (C2). The first DoD C-sUAS strategy followed in [January 2021](https://media.defense.gov/2021/Jan/07/2002561080/-1/-1/0/DEPARTMENT-OF-DEFENSE-COUNTER-SMALL-UNMANNED-AIRCRAFT-SYSTEMS-STRATEGY.pdf).

Newcomers arrived in this period:
- **Anduril** won a [DIU agreement worth up to $99M (Jul 2021)](https://www.ocbj.com/technology/anduril-lands-dod-contract-drone-protection-99m-wi/) for installation C-UAS across all services, then SOCOM's [CUxS integrator IDIQ ($967.6M, Jan 2022)](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2022/01/20/anduril-receives-967-million-idiq-contract-for-counter-unmanned-systems/), later raised to $1.92B.
- **High-power microwave:** AFRL's THOR led to Leidos's [Mjölnir ($26.9M, Feb 2022)](https://www.afrl.af.mil/News/Article/2945744/afrl-awards-contract-for-drone-killer-mjlnir-brings-new-drone-hammer-to-the-fig/).
- **Air Force base defense** support contracts (2021, from USAspending): SAIC $119M, Leidos $86M, Booz Allen $83M (C2, RF, interceptors), CACI $81M.
- **Europe:** France's [PARADE (€350M, Apr 2022)](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2022/04/28/thales-cs-group-team-to-build-new-counter-drone-systems-for-french-military/) for the Rugby World Cup and the Olympics. The UK bought [Rafael's Drone Dome (£15.8M, 2018)](https://www.timesofisrael.com/uk-army-said-to-use-israeli-made-system-to-end-drone-chaos-at-london-airport/).

## Phase 3 — The Ukraine and Red Sea effect (2022–2024)

Russia's invasion turned drones into mass weapons. Shahed/Geran launches rose from about 200 a week to [about 1,100 a week](https://www.csis.org/analysis/drone-saturation-russias-shahed-campaign). Western buying followed:

- **For Ukraine:** L3Harris [VAMPIRE in a $3B US aid package (Aug 2022)](https://defensescoop.com/2022/08/24/new-3-billion-us-military-aid-package-for-ukraine-includes-counter-drone-systems/); Germany paid for [Rheinmetall Skynex (€182M, Dec 2022)](https://www.thedefensepost.com/2022/12/16/germany-rheinmetall-air-defense-ukraine/).
- **FMS:** Qatar's [FS-LIDS package ($1B, Nov 2022)](https://breakingdefense.com/2022/12/us-approves-potential-1-billion-counter-drone-tech-sale-to-qatar/), including 200 Coyote Block 2.
- **Directed energy matures:** Epirus Leonidas wins the Army HPM shoot-off ([$66.1M, Jan 2023](https://www.epirusinc.com/news-item/us-army-awards-epirus-661m-contract-for-leonidas-directed-energy-system)); BlueHalo's LOCUST AMP-HEL ($45.7M + options, Apr 2023); South Korea's [Block-I laser into production (~$72.5M, Jun 2024)](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/koreas-star-wars-era-begins-as-laser-drone-killer-enters-mass-production/10783472); Israel's [Iron Beam (NIS 2B, Oct 2024)](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-defense-ministry-signs-nis-2b-laser-defense-systems-deal-1001492483).
- **Coyote scales:** $237M (2023), 600 Block 2C for $75M (Jan 2024), $197M (Sep 2024).
- **Guns return:** Germany's [Skyranger 30 (€595M, Feb 2024)](https://thedefensepost.com/2024/02/28/germany-boxer-mounted-skyranger-turret/), then Austria, Denmark, Hungary and the Netherlands.

Two events reset US priorities. In December 2023, unidentified drones [overflew Langley AFB for 17 days](https://www.twz.com/air/mysterious-drones-swarmed-langley-afb-for-weeks%20). On 28 January 2024, a one-way drone [killed three US soldiers at Tower 22 in Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tower_22_drone_attack).

**Interceptor drones enter US buying** at the same time: Anduril's [Roadrunner-M ($250M, Oct 2024)](https://www.defensenews.com/unmanned/2024/10/08/anduril-lands-250-million-pentagon-contract-for-drone-defense-system/) and the [Marine Corps CES with Anvil ($200M, Nov 2024)](https://insideunmannedsystems.com/anduril-wins-200-million-contract-to-develop-cuas-for-the-marine-air-defense-integrated-system-madis/). Replicator 2 was redirected to [installation C-sUAS (Sep 2024)](https://news.usni.org/2024/09/30/stopping-lethal-attack-drones-is-focus-of-replicator-initiative-2-says-secdef-austin).

## Phase 4 — Homeland and scale (2025)

- **Marine Corps installations:** [Anduril I-CsUAS, $642M (Mar 2025)](https://defensescoop.com/2025/03/13/marine-corps-anduril-contract-defend-installations-small-uas-drones/).
- **M&A:** AeroVironment bought BlueHalo for [$4.1B (May 2025)](https://dronexl.co/2025/10/22/aerovironment-nabs-96-million-army-contract). Axon had [completed its purchase of Dedrone in October 2024](https://www.securitysales.com/news/axon-completes-acquisition-of-dedrone/162133/).
- **India–Pakistan (May 2025)** led to India's [$231.6M emergency procurement](https://www.defensenews.com/global/asia-pacific/2025/06/30/india-buys-drones-air-defense-weapons-following-aerial-pakistan-fight/).
- **Operation Spiderweb (1 Jun 2025):** Ukraine launched 117 drones from trucks inside Russia and hit strategic bombers. It's the scenario base commanders now plan against.
- **Policy:** [EO 14305 (Jun 2025)](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/06/restoring-american-airspace-sovereignty/) created a federal airspace-sovereignty task force; the reconciliation act funded [$500M FEMA C-UAS grants](https://www.fema.gov/grants/preparedness/counter-unmanned-aircraft-systems-grant-program); Hegseth created [JIATF-401 (Aug 2025)](https://defensescoop.com/2025/08/28/hegseth-army-new-counter-drone-task-force-jiatf-401/) to replace the JCO.
- **Europe gets hit:** Russian drones crossed into [Poland (Sep 2025)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Russian_drone_incursion_into_Poland), and Denmark's airports and bases saw incursions.
- **The biggest LIDS award:** Raytheon [Coyote, $5.04B (Sep 2025)](https://news.clearancejobs.com/2025/09/29/raytheon-lands-5b-army-contract-for-coyote-missile-defense-systems/).

## What history says about UM's path

1. **Every era's winners first proved themselves in a test or in combat.** SRC and Raytheon via JUONs, Epirus by beating six rivals, Anduril through DIU, Merops in Ukraine.
2. **Early integrators became gatekeepers.** FAAD-C2 then and Lattice now: being integrated has always been a condition of being bought.
3. **Homeland rules have always lagged battlefield tech.** 130i (2016), 124n (2018), SAFER SKIES (2025). Capture and low-collateral effectors have been the homeland answer at each step.
